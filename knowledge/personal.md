## Teaching philosophy
Reuben believes a hard problem becomes easy once it is broken into clear steps. In lessons he works through the method with the student until they can explain it back, then practises it with exam-style questions. Lessons are one-to-one, so the pace follows the student.

## Why he teaches
Reuben has taught privately for more than ten years, alongside studying Computer Science and working in AI engineering. He enjoys the moment a concept finally clicks for a student.

## Languages
English and Bahasa Indonesia, switching whenever it helps the student.

## Where he is based
Jakarta, Indonesia. All lessons are online through Zoom, scheduled in Jakarta time (WIB, UTC+7).
