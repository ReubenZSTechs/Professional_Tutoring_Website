# Subjects and levels

Each subject also has its own page on the website listing every topic covered.

## Mathematics (school)
Reuben teaches school Mathematics at these levels:
- Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580) and IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606)
- Cambridge O Level Mathematics (4024)
- Cambridge International AS and A Level Mathematics (9709)
- IB Mathematics

## IGCSE and O Level Mathematics topics
Number, algebra and graphs, coordinate geometry, geometry and mensuration, trigonometry (including the sine and cosine rules), vectors and transformations, probability, and statistics.

## Additional Mathematics topics
Functions, quadratics, polynomials (factor and remainder theorems), equations and inequalities, logarithms and exponentials, straight-line graphs, circular measure, trigonometric identities and equations, permutations and combinations, series (binomial expansion, arithmetic and geometric progressions), vectors, and differentiation and integration.

## AS and A Level Mathematics topics
Pure Mathematics: quadratics, functions, coordinate geometry, circular measure, trigonometry, series, differentiation and integration, then logarithms, further trigonometry, further calculus, numerical methods, vectors, differential equations and complex numbers. Also Probability and Statistics, and Mechanics.

## IB Mathematics topics
Number and algebra, functions, geometry and trigonometry, statistics and probability, and calculus.

## University Mathematics
Reuben teaches university-level mathematics to university students, at Rp 375,000 per 60-minute session. Subjects covered:
- Calculus I: limits, continuity, derivatives and their applications, and an introduction to integrals.
- Calculus II: integration techniques, applications of integrals, sequences and series, Taylor series, and parametric and polar curves.
- Calculus III (multivariable calculus): partial derivatives, gradients, multiple integrals, and vector calculus including Green's, Stokes' and the divergence theorems.
- Linear Algebra: systems of equations, matrices, determinants, vector spaces, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and diagonalisation.
- Discrete Mathematics: logic and proofs, sets, relations and functions, combinatorics, graph theory, and recurrence relations.
- Advanced differentiation: differentiating with respect to vectors and matrices, gradients, Jacobians and Hessians, as used in optimisation and machine learning.
- Integration: advanced techniques such as substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions and improper integrals.
- Differential Equations: first-order and second-order linear equations, systems of equations, and Laplace transforms.
- Partial Differential Equations: classification, separation of variables, the heat, wave and Laplace equations, and Fourier series.

## Physics
Reuben teaches Physics for Cambridge IGCSE (0625) and IB.
- IGCSE topics: motion, forces and energy; thermal physics; waves; electricity and magnetism; nuclear physics; and space physics.
- IB topics: space, time and motion; the particulate nature of matter; wave behaviour; fields; and nuclear and quantum physics.

## Chemistry
Reuben teaches Chemistry for Cambridge IGCSE (0620) and IB.
- IGCSE topics: states of matter; atoms, elements and compounds; stoichiometry and moles; electrochemistry; chemical energetics; rates of reaction and equilibrium; acids, bases and salts; the periodic table; metals; chemistry of the environment; organic chemistry; and experimental techniques.
- IB topics: models of the particulate nature of matter, bonding and structure, the classification of matter, energetics, rates and equilibrium, reaction mechanisms, and organic chemistry.
