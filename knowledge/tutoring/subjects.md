# Subjects and levels

## Mathematics
Reuben teaches Mathematics at these levels:
- Cambridge IGCSE Mathematics (0580) and IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606)
- Cambridge O Level Mathematics (4024)
- Cambridge International AS and A Level Mathematics (9709)
- IB Mathematics

Topics range from algebra and geometry to calculus and statistics, with exam-style practice on every topic.

## Physics
Reuben teaches Physics for Cambridge IGCSE (0625) and IB. Lessons cover forces, energy, waves and electricity step by step, with plenty of worked problems.

## Chemistry
Reuben teaches Chemistry for Cambridge IGCSE (0620) and IB. Lessons cover atomic structure, bonding and reactions, plus clear methods for calculations such as moles and concentration.

## Computer Science and Python (coming soon)
Computer Science (Cambridge IGCSE 0478) and beginner Python programming lessons are planned but not yet open for booking. Parents who are interested can message Reuben to be told when they start.

## Subjects not offered
Subjects and levels not listed above are not currently offered. If you are unsure whether a subject or level is covered, ask Reuben directly on WhatsApp.
