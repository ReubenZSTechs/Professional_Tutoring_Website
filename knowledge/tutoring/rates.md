# Lesson rates

## Rates per session
All rates are in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) for one 60-minute private online lesson.

| Level | Rate per 60-minute session |
| --- | --- |
| IGCSE and O Level | Rp 225,000 |
| AS and A Level | Rp 275,000 |
| IB | Rp 325,000 |
| University mathematics | Rp 375,000 |

The rate depends on the level only. For school levels it is the same for Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry. University mathematics covers subjects such as Calculus I, II and III, Linear Algebra, Discrete Mathematics, Differential Equations and Partial Differential Equations.

## Recommended number of lessons
Two sessions per week are recommended for steady progress. As an example, two IGCSE sessions a week cost Rp 450,000 per week.

## Discounts and packages
There are no discounts, packages or bundle prices. Every lesson is charged at the per-session rate above.

## Free consultation
Before committing to lessons, there is a free 40-minute Zoom consultation with the parent and the student. It is used to understand where the student is, agree on goals and choose a schedule. There is no charge and no obligation to continue.
