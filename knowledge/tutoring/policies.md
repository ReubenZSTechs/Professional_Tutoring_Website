# Scheduling and policies

## Scheduling
Lesson times are agreed with each family during the free consultation and are set in Jakarta time (WIB, UTC+7). Available time slots change, so ask Reuben on WhatsApp for current availability.

## Rescheduling and cancellations
For rescheduling or cancelling a lesson, contact Reuben on WhatsApp as early as possible. The details are agreed with each family during the consultation.

## Privacy
Details sent through the enquiry form are only used to reply to the enquiry.
