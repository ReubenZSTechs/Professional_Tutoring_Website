# Frequently asked questions

## How do lessons work?
Lessons are private, one-to-one and online through Zoom. Each lesson is 60 minutes. Lessons work through the student's school syllabus topic by topic, with exam-style questions.

## How do we get started?
1. Get in touch through WhatsApp or the enquiry form on the website, with the subject, level and any upcoming exam date.
2. Join a free 40-minute Zoom consultation with the parent and the student. Reuben finds out where the student is, agrees goals with you and suggests a schedule.
3. Start regular lessons at an agreed time. Two sessions per week are recommended.

## Are lessons in English or Bahasa Indonesia?
Either, or a mix of both. Reuben uses whichever helps the student understand best.

## Are lessons online or in person?
All lessons are online through Zoom. There are no in-person or home-visit lessons.

## Which time zone are lessons in?
Lessons are scheduled in Jakarta time (WIB, UTC+7). Students within about three hours of Jakarta time are welcome, for example in Singapore, Malaysia, Brunei, the Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, Western Australia, India and the UAE, as well as all Indonesian time zones (WIB, WITA, WIT).

## What does my child need for online lessons?
A laptop or tablet with Zoom installed and a stable internet connection.

## How much do lessons cost?
Per 60-minute session: Rp 225,000 for IGCSE and O Level, Rp 275,000 for AS and A Level, Rp 325,000 for IB, and Rp 375,000 for university mathematics. There are no discounts or packages. The first consultation of 40 minutes is free.

## How many lessons per week are recommended?
Two sessions per week are recommended.

## Is there a trial lesson?
There is a free 40-minute Zoom consultation with the parent and the student before committing to lessons.

## Are group lessons available?
Lessons are private and one-to-one. For anything else, ask Reuben directly on WhatsApp.
