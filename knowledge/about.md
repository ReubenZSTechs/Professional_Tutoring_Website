# About Reuben Susanto Tutoring

## Who Reuben is
Reuben Zachary Susanto is a private tutor based in Jakarta, Indonesia. He teaches Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry to students following the Cambridge (IGCSE, O Level, AS and A Level) and IB programmes, and university-level mathematics to university students. He has more than 10 years of private teaching experience.

Reuben is a final-year Computer Science student at Calvin Institute of Technology, specialising in AI Engineering, with a GPA of 3.90 out of 4.0. He was named on the Rector's List in 2024 and 2025.

## Who the lessons are for
Lessons are for students at international schools and national-plus schools who are preparing for IGCSE, O Level, AS/A Level or IB exams. Most enquiries come from parents choosing a tutor for their child, and students are welcome to get in touch themselves. University students can book lessons in university mathematics.

## Teaching approach
- Lessons are one-to-one, so the pace and the topics follow the individual student.
- Lessons follow the syllabus the student is studying at school, topic by topic.
- Every topic includes exam-style questions, so students know what to expect in the real exam.
- Reuben explains in English or Bahasa Indonesia, and can switch between the two whenever that helps a concept make sense.
- His background in computer science and AI engineering gives lessons a structured, step-by-step, problem-solving style.

## Languages
Lessons are taught in English, in Bahasa Indonesia, or in a mix of both.
